    06 Method, and the honest caveats

    How it works

    Your paste is split into turns, the ones you wrote are isolated, and each axis counts phrase events in your own words: apologies, courtesies, accusations, near-duplicate re-asks (measured with token-set overlap), constraints, median length, compliments, chaos markers, and urgency words. Verdicts are a weighted fit over those nine axes, resolved by a fixed table in js/archetypes.js.

    Why there is no leaderboard

    A leaderboard would need a server, a database, and your messages. That is the trade this site refuses to make. Results are not stored, not counted, and not seen by anyone — including whoever built it.

    Where each bar reaches 100

    Saturation point for each of the nine axes

    What this is not

    Not a psychological assessment, not a diagnostic tool, and not evidence of anything. It is a phrase counter with opinions. Small samples produce confident nonsense — paste at least 20 of your messages before you argue with the result.

    Cost, stated plainly

    Static files on a CDN. No functions, no database, no third-party APIs, no per-request compute. If this page gets ten million visits, the bill is still zero dollars, which is the only reason it can afford to be honest about your prompts.
